# Changelog

## 1.0.1

- **FoF Forum Widgets: "Your journey" is now placed in FoF's layout editor.** It is listed there as an available widget; drag it into a section and save. It used to register with a section name FoF does not have, so it could never be drawn, and the stock sidebar card stood down whenever FoF was installed, so members on a forum with FoF saw no journey card at all. Now the stock sidebar card shows unless the widget is placed, and the placed widget shows only for a member with an open journey.

## 1.0.0

First release: onboarding steps that tick themselves, 21 step types, rewards in FoF Badges badges and FoF Gamification points, the launcher, the journey page, reminders, the step editor and the funnel.
